Drawing Structural Formulas of Amines

Select a problem from the list on the right. When the Show Problem button is pressed a common or IUPAC name of a compound will be shown in the information window. Draw the structural formula for this compound in the drawing window. If you need to draw a second species use the New button for this purpose. Specific configurations must be drawn with the wedge tool. Your answer is evaluated by the Check Answer button.
