Drawing Structural Formulas of Alkynes

Select a problem from the list on the right. When the Show Problem button is pressed the IUPAC name of a compound will be shown in the information window. Draw the structural formula for this compound in the drawing window. Stereoisomers involving the configuration of ring substituents should be drawn using the wedge drawing tool. Your answer is evaluated by the Check Answer button.
