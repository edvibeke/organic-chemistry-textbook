Drawing Structural Formulas of Alcohols

Select a problem from the list on the right. When the Show Problem button is pressed the IUPAC name of a compound will be shown in the information window. Draw the structural formula for this compound in the drawing window. Use the wedge drawing tool to designate stereoisomeric ring substituents. Your answer is evaluated by the Check Answer button.
