Products from Reactions of Epoxides

Draw the major organic product from each of the reactions shown below. You do not need to draw or indicate any inorganic ionic products.
To draw a formula use the drawing window opened by the Draw Formulas button. Do not draw hydrogen atoms or attempt to show configuration. When you are finished, Submit your formula and then check it by pressing the Check Product button. A help screen is available for the drawing window.
The "View Answers" button will only function after you have made at least three efforts to answer these questions.
