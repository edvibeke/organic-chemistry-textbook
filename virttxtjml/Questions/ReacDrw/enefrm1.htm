Reactions of Alkenes
In this problem we shall consider eight alkene reactants:
4-methyl-1-pentene     trans-3-hexene     cis-3-hexene     methylenecyclopentane    
1-methylcyclopentene     2,3-dimethyl-2-butene     indene     ethylidenecyclohexane    

These compounds may be reacted with nine different reagents:
Hydrogen chloride, HCl     Bromine, Br2     (i) Hg(OAc)2 + H2O
    (ii) NaBH4        
Potassium Permanganate, KMnO4     H2   (Pd or Pt catalyst)     Hypobromous Acid, HOBr    
(i) Diborane, B2H6    
    (ii) H2O2 in NaOH soln.     		HBr   (peroxide initiator)     Perbenzoic Acid, C6H5CO3H    

Select one alkene from the first group,
and one reagent from the second group.
