Identifying Functional Groups

The compounds whose structures are shown below, incorporate a variety of functional groups. The questions on the right ask you to identify which compounds have a specific functional group. For each compound that has the designated group, enter the appropriate number (shown in green) in the answer box. The aromatic rings should not be counted as double bonds.
Do not enter superfluous characters, since they will be counted as incorrect answers.
