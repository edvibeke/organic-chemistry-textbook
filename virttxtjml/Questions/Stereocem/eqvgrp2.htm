Structurally Equivalent Atoms & Groups

For each of the compounds A through F indicate the number of structurally-distinct groups of carbon atoms, and also the number of distinct groups of constitutionally equivalent hydrogens. Stereochemically distinct hydrogens should not be reported. Enter a number from 1 to 9 in each answer box.
A three-dimensional model for each compound will be displayed by clicking on its structure or letter designation. The window in which the model is displayed may be moved to suit the viewer. Remember, many of these compounds are conformationally mobile, and the model depicts only one conformer.
